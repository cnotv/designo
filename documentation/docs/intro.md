# Designo

A personal, self-hosted design tool in the spirit of Figma, with its own MCP server, so you and
your agents can design without usage limits. Your files live on your disk or your own server.

![The Designo plan: architecture, MCP tools, milestones and risks](/img/plan.svg)

- **Run it:** [self-hosting](./guides/self-hosting.md), with Docker.
- **Connect an agent:** [MCP setup](./guides/mcp.md).
- **How it fits together:** [architecture](./architecture.md), and what comes next in the
  [roadmap](./roadmap.md).
